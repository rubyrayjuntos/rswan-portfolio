# Kitchen Kontrol — adoption outcomes

These figures are the product story used on the resume and this case study. They are operational results, not model-leaderboard scores.

| Measure | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reporting time | 30 minutes **per day** | 15 minutes **per week** |
| Compliance | (paper / incomplete) | 95% |
| Food waste | (uncontrolled) | 3% |
| Sites in the product story | — | 23 school nutrition sites |

LinkedIn has also described a 27-school pilot. This page uses **23** to match the product-manager and architect resumes until those numbers are reconciled.

## What “success” meant

- Frontline staff complete logs during service
- District managers see weekly status without collecting clipboards
- Inventory and milk tracking reduce over-order and expiration dump

AI is justified only where it moves these numbers: faster legal logs, fewer missed CCPs, better forecast, fewer wasted pans.
