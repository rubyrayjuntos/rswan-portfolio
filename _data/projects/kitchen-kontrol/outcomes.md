# Kitchen Kontrol — outcomes (honest bound)

Earlier resume and product copy used these figures:

| Measure | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reporting time | 30 minutes **per day** | 15 minutes **per week** |
| Compliance | (paper / incomplete) | 95% |
| Food waste | (uncontrolled) | 3% |
| Sites | — | 23 (LinkedIn has also said 27) |

**These numbers are not in the GitHub repos.** They are operational story from cafeteria work that was not transferred into source. This case study does not treat them as measured proof.

## What GitHub can prove

- A JWT-authenticated ops API with HACCP log templates, assignments, submissions, and weekly completion SQL
- Ajv-validated forms and TFER-style range checks
- Planogram persistence
- Docker, migrations, Jest/Cypress, GitHub Actions
- A separate browser-STT milk logger
- A public HACCP SPA and a public HTML hub

## What “success” still means as a product goal

- Frontline staff complete logs during service
- District managers see weekly status without collecting clipboards
- Inventory and milk tracking reduce over-order and expiration dump

Until those metrics are produced from shipped code or a named pilot with artifacts, they stay off the card.
